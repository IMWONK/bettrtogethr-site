Why I built this

I built the place I wanted.

I have ADHD. Small daily tasks slip past me unless they become habits. I couldn't find a free app that tracked my workouts and my habits in one place, and every workout tracker logged my sets and then left me there.

I also don't post on social media. It feels like everything has to look perfect, and saying the wrong thing gets you shut out.

So I built the place I wanted: simple, private by default, and full of people who notice when you do the small stuff.

The idea
You become who your habits and your environment make you. Most apps only work on the habits.
BETTR TOGETHR is also the environment.
Togethr

Your friends don't have to get it. These people do.

See what people are working on and cheer them on. Send a friend request to someone heading the same direction. Post under your name or anonymously. No influencers and no showing off, just people who are trying. Everything you share is something you chose to share.

Wins

The small stuff counts here.

Made your bed. Drank water before coffee. Write it down. Keep a win to yourself, share it with friends, or share it with everyone. Either way, the app makes a moment of it.

Habits

Habits without the guilt.

Start by writing down why you're here. It stays on your Home screen, and every version you write is kept.

Mark habits done as you go. Miss a day and nothing turns red. There's no streak to break. Pick it back up whenever.

Workouts

Last time: 135 for 8. Today: 9?

  • Log set by set, with only the fields the exercise uses: weight, reps, distance, time or effort.
  • See your last workout, so you can push a little more.
  • Personal records on your lifts are found for you.
  • Repeat a past workout in three taps.
Journal

A journal that stays yours.

Gratitude entries are private by default. Reflections can never be shared at all. Nothing you write is shown to advertisers or used to train AI.

Progress

See how far you've come.

Progress without judgment: how consistent you've been, what you've lifted, and the milestones you've passed. A new Bettr of the Day waits on Home every morning.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is it free?

Yes. BETTR TOGETHR is free to download and use, with no ads.

What devices does it run on?

iPhone, through the App Store. There's no Android version right now.

Is it available everywhere?

Not yet. BETTR TOGETHR isn't available in the EU at launch.

Do I have to use the social side?

No. Habits, workouts, your journal and your wins all work on their own. The Togethr feed is there when you want company.

Who can see what I write?

Only the people you choose. Your journal is private by default, reflections can never be shared, and every win lets you pick: just you, friends, or everyone.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing. There's no streak counter and nothing turns red. You pick up where you left off.

Do you sell my data or use it to train AI?

No. We don't track you or sell your data. There are no ads and no analytics or advertising code in the app. Your writing is never used to train AI.

Can I get my data out, or delete my account?

Yes, both from inside the app. Deleting your account starts a 30-day recovery window, then everything is permanently erased.

How is the community kept safe?

The text of every post and comment is screened before it publishes. Anything can be reported, any user can be blocked, and a real person reviews every report.

How old do I have to be?

The App Store rates BETTR TOGETHR 13+.

Is this therapy or medical advice?

No. It's not medical or psychological advice and it's not a crisis service. It's a tool for the ordinary work of getting a little better.

How do I get help?

Email support@bettrtogethr.net. It reaches the person who built the app, and they read it.
